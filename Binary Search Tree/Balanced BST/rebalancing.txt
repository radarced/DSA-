this file contains an explanation of the principles that the rebalance function
works upon .

in a balanced bst deletion or insertion always leaves the nodes of the subtree with a balance
factor of |2|.
^ ig its just because of the nature of the bbst and how deletion operations are done on them .

insertion / deletion ( the only tree manipulating functions ) would cause the nodes that are
related to the lastNode that was "manipulated" to be unbalanced everything else remains untouched.

in other words the only nodes that are affected and could be unbalanced are those that are on
the depth path of the lastInvolvedNode .

for a bst to be balanced .
- both left and right subtrees must be bbst's themselves .
- the balance factor of the root node must not be |2|.
